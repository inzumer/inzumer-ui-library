`Modal` sizes are aligned: 90% of the viewport width up to 768px, as tall as the content up to 80% of the viewport height (new `maxHeight="tall"` allows 90%). Title and footer stay in place and the content scrolls inside; the footer wraps its buttons on narrow screens. `CookieConsent`'s preferences follow the same size.
