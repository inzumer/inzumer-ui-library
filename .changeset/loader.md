New `Loader`: a spinning or pulsing mark (a ring by default, or your brand's logo in `mark`) with an accessible label, sizes, speeds and inline or stacked layout; with reduced motion it pulses slowly instead of spinning. `Button` gets a `loading` prop: disabled, `aria-busy` and pulsing while it waits. Entry point `./loader`.
