`Carousel`: new `buttons`, `buttonsPosition` and `indicatorsPosition` props (`start`, `center` or `end`). When buttons and indicators share a side they go together; otherwise each sits on its side of the same row. The buttons are now round, in the primary color, with the components' shadow and chevron icons, and the indicator dots use the primary color (the current one full, the rest faded).
